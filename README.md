# Acme Insurance, corporate website

Acme Insurance is a European insurance company with around 2,000 employees, headquartered in Lyon, France.

This repository will hold the backend for our corporate website.

## Context

- The website is available in English and French
- It has standard pages (Home, About, Careers, Contact), a blog, and a team page
- Content is managed by our marketing team; legal and compliance must review content before it goes live
- Our engineering team works in TypeScript
- For compliance reasons, everything must be self-hosted on our own infrastructure
